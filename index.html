About Me

I'm a game developer and music producer from Chile. I spend my days building gameplay and interface systems, and the rest of my time composing the soundtracks that go with them. To me, a game feels finished when the way it plays and the way it sounds are telling the same story.

On the technical side, I program in Unity with C# and C++, design adaptive audio with FMOD, produce original music in FL Studio, and model 3D assets in Blender. Game jams, university projects, and professional commissions have taught me to wear several hats on a small team and to ship under tight deadlines without losing sight of the player's experience.

Away from the keyboard, I'm usually playing indie games, experimenting with new synth sounds, or taking apart a favorite soundtrack to understand why it works. I'm happy working on anything from fast-paced arcade games to therapeutic serious games, and I'm always looking for teams who care about craft, curiosity, and good audio as much as I do.
